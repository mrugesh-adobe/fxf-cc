# PRODUCT — Target (FedEx Custom Critical Owner/Operator)

_Target strategy for the consolidated Owner/Operator Opportunities page.
Authored by `stardust:direct`. Mode A (brand-faithful)._

## Register

`brand` — a recruitment / marketing landing page for prospective and
current independent contractors.

## Users

- **Prospective owner operators** — own or plan to buy a truck; evaluating
  contracting with FedEx Custom Critical.
- **Prospective CDL drivers** — seeking fleet-driver opportunities.
- **Current fleet contractors** — returning for advantages, communications,
  online tools, and answers to common questions.

## Product Purpose

Present the FedEx Custom Critical owner-operator opportunity as one coherent
page — the intro, the advantages (Programs / Communications / Online Tools),
the frequently asked questions, and a clear path to apply — and route
visitors to call or apply. Consolidates four legacy pages into one.

## Brand Personality

- `civic-formal` — established, trustworthy, corporate FedEx.
- `institutional` — the weight of a national brand behind an independent
  contractor's decision.
- Direct and benefit-led toward a working-driver audience: plain language,
  obvious next steps.

## Anti-references

- The legacy template itself (see `stardust/prototypes/owneroperator-improvements.md`):
  Times-New-Roman-over-Roboto headings, ad-hoc type scale, default-blue vs
  teal links, fragmented multi-page IA, a leaked SSI error string.
- **Generic-2026-SaaS silhouette guardrail** — "modernise" must not mean a
  centered hero + dual-pill-CTA + three-feature-card wash. Stay recognisably
  FedEx: purple, orange, plain-spoken, utilitarian.

## Design Principles

- **Stay unmistakably FedEx** — purple `#4D148C` + orange `#FF6600` anchor
  every surface; nothing generic.
- **One page, one read** — intro → advantages tabs → FAQ accordion → contact,
  in a single scroll, no reloads.
- **Type does the hierarchy** — one family (Roboto), a real modular scale,
  weight and size (not serif/sans mixing) carry structure.
- **Clarity for a working audience** — scannable tabs and accordion, one
  consistent link treatment, obvious call-to-apply.
- **Content faithful** — all copy is reproduced verbatim from the source;
  this is a restructure, not a rewrite.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast on every text-on-ground pair.
  - Accepted exception (2026-09-25): white labels on FedEx orange `#FF6600`
    primary buttons (2.94:1), kept to match FedEx's own button treatment.
- Tabs and accordion keyboard-operable with visible focus.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.
