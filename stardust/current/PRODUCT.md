# PRODUCT — Current State (descriptive)

_Descriptive snapshot of the existing FedEx Custom Critical Owner/Operator
site area. Authored by `stardust:extract`; describes what the site **is**,
not what it should become._

## Register

`brand` — a marketing / recruitment surface aimed at prospective and
current independent contractors. Not a transactional product UI.

## Users

- **Prospective owner operators** — individuals who own (or plan to buy) a
  truck and are evaluating contracting with FedEx Custom Critical.
- **Prospective CDL drivers** — drivers looking for fleet-driver
  opportunities.
- **Current fleet contractors** — existing owner operators returning for
  program details, communications, and extranet/tools access.

## Product Purpose

Explain the owner-operator opportunity at FedEx Custom Critical and route
visitors toward applying — surfacing advantages (reward programs,
communications, online tools), qualifications/specifications by vehicle
class, and answers to common questions (FAQs).

## Brand Personality

Established, trustworthy, and utilitarian. Corporate FedEx identity (purple
+ orange) applied to a plain, information-dense recruitment site. Tone is
direct and benefit-led ("Learn why FedEx Custom Critical is a great
opportunity for owner operators"), speaking plainly to a working-driver
audience.

## Anti-references

_provenance: inferred — basis: the captured page's visible weaknesses._

- The legacy template itself: Times-New-Roman headings on a Roboto body,
  ad-hoc type scale, undifferentiated text-link lists, an unstyled server
  error string leaking into the page ("[an error occurred while processing
  this directive]"), and tab/section content split across multiple `.shtml`
  pages. The redesign should not reproduce this dated, fragmented feel.

## Design Principles

_provenance: inferred — basis: brand surface + captured content._

- **FedEx brand fidelity** — purple `#4D148C` + orange remain the anchor.
- **Clarity for a working audience** — plain language, obvious next steps
  (call, apply), scannable structure.
- **Consolidation** — the current experience is spread across a homepage,
  an overview/advantages page (tabbed: Programs / Communications / Online
  Tools), and a FAQs page; the redesign target is a single coherent page.
