Owner/Operator Opportunities

The FedEx Custom Critical fleet is made up entirely of owner operators. If you own your vehicle, or have plans to purchase one, read on to learn about your opportunity as an independent contractor.

As North America's largest time-specific, critical-shipment carrier, FedEx Custom Critical offers owner operators excellent earning potential. We're open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year and have no scheduled runs.

Advantages

Explore the programs, communications, and online tools that support our fleet.

We recognize the members of our fleet for great customer service and safe driving through a number of reward programs.

  • Annual Elite Fleet Award

    This award goes to the top 40 owner operators in the fleet each year, based on availability, load acceptance, on-time service and accident-free driving. The winners receive an all-expenses-paid trip with an awards gala in their honor.

  • Quarterly Elite Fleet Award

    At the end of each quarter, we will look at all the contractors who meet the Four Star criteria and will award $100 savings bonds to the 100 with the highest load acceptance for that quarter.

  • One Year Accident Free

    The accident free program requires an individual to be active the entire calendar year beginning with 1/1 of that year until 12/31 and have no involvement in any preventable accidents.

  • 10- and 20-Year Safe Driving Awards

    This award honors our owner operators who drive accident-free with us for 10 or 20 years.

We offer clear communications from our headquarters to you out on the road.

  • In-House Staff Dedicated to You

    Our Dispatchers work with you to get freight for your truck and can help get day-to-day questions answered.

  • Contractor Relations

    Our Contractor Relations department deals exclusively with our fleet, from negotiating discounts to answering questions about your lease agreement.

  • Safety & Settlements

    Our Safety department is dedicated to ensuring we have the safest fleet possible. And our Contractor Settlements team ensures you are paid weekly and on time.

  • Around the Clock Support

    Speak to a fleet specialist 24/7/365 or use our The Voice Response Unit (VRU) to access the information you need to run your business.

The owner-operator extranet is a Web site for FedEx Custom Critical independent contractors only. This password-protected site features:

  • Up-to-date vehicle information. See where your trucks are right now.
  • Information on completed runs, including revenue generated.
  • Settlement sheets available up to one week before you receive them in the mail.
  • Capability to download or order needed paperwork and supplies online.
  • The policies and procedures you need to know. Save a phone call and get all your questions answered online.

The extranet features all the tools you need to effectively manage your business. And if you can't find the answer you are looking for, the site features email capabilities to reach whatever department you need at headquarters.

A FedEx Custom Critical load board allows you to have greater visibility into the loads that are available and will also provide more freedom to choose the loads you want.

Safety takes priority at FedEx Custom Critical, and we're looking for the best professional owner operators. Our general minimum requirements are:

  • No more than three moving violations within the last three years, and one or less in the previous 12 months
  • Ability to pass a Department of Transportation physical and drug test
  • A proper class CDL with HAZMAT endorsement
  • Unrestricted access to and from Canada

In order to contract with or drive for a contractor of FedEx Custom Critical, drivers must meet certain other eligibility requirements. These vary based on the size and type of vehicle: small straight truck, large straight truck, and tractor-trailer.
